Exhibit A — Media & Subscriptions

Netflix User Analytics

SQL · PYTHON · POWER BI
  • Dataset: Streaming and subscription records across multiple countries, devices and subscription tiers (synthetic, portfolio dataset)
  • Problem: No single read on what was driving revenue and engagement across plans, devices and countries.
  • Approach: SQL for the business questions, Pandas for cleaning/EDA, Power BI for the interactive dashboard.
  • Insight: Premium is the clear revenue leader among the three subscription tiers, well ahead of Standard and Basic combined.
  • Insight: WiFi is the dominant connection type for streaming sessions, ahead of broadband and mobile data.
  • Recommendation: Test a Standard→Premium upsell flow in the top-performing markets, and prioritize streaming-performance improvements for WiFi and mobile, the two heaviest-used connection types.
  • Result: Interactive KPI dashboard (revenue, ARPU, watch time, top titles) built for retention & upsell targeting.

Exhibit B — Healthcare Operations

LifeLine Hospital Analytics

SQL · PYTHON · POWER BI
  • Dataset: Patient records covering admissions, billing, insurance, diagnoses and doctor assignments (synthetic, portfolio dataset)
  • Problem: No consolidated view of admissions, revenue, doctor performance or bed occupancy for management.
  • Approach: SQL views & queries for cleaning/analysis, Python for EDA, Power BI for the executive dashboard.
  • Insight: Hypertension is the single most common diagnosis by patient volume, ahead of Diabetes and Pneumonia.
  • Insight: General Ward carries by far the largest share of bed occupancy, well above Semi-Private, Private Room and ICU.
  • Recommendation: Review General Ward capacity and staffing first, and consider a fast-track intake process for the top diagnoses by volume, since small efficiency gains there scale across the most patients.
  • Result: Executive KPI summary (patients, revenue, insurance amount, avg billing, avg stay) for hospital management.

Exhibit C — Urban Mobility

Rapido Ride Analytics

SQL · PYTHON · POWER BI
  • Dataset: Ride-booking records covering bookings, cancellations, fares and ratings across vehicle types (synthetic, portfolio dataset)
  • Problem: No clear read on demand patterns, cancellations or revenue across vehicle types and locations.
  • Approach: SQL for the business questions, Python for EDA, Power BI for an executive-style dashboard.
  • Insight: "Cancelled by Driver" is the single largest cause of failed bookings, ahead of customer-side cancellations and "driver not found" cases.
  • Insight: Identified the most-booked vehicle type and the top pickup/drop locations by demand.
  • Recommendation: Since driver-side cancellations outweigh customer-side, investigate driver availability and matching-radius in the highest-cancellation locations and time windows before touching pricing.
  • Result: Dashboard tracking total bookings, revenue, and completed-vs-cancelled rides for operational review.

Exhibit D — Retail & E-commerce

Superstore Sales Analysis

SQL · PYTHON · POWER BI
  • Dataset: Kaggle's Sample Superstore dataset — real public retail data (orders, customers, products, regions)
  • Problem: Needed clarity on which categories, regions and products actually drove profit, not just sales volume.
  • Approach: SQL + Python for analysis, one Power BI dashboard covering sales, profit and customer views.
  • Insight: Technology is the top-performing category by sales, ahead of Furniture and Office Supplies.
  • Insight: Ranked the top products and customers by sales, and flagged the loss-making products.
  • Recommendation: With loss-making products already flagged, the next step is checking whether high discounts are driving those losses — cap discount depth on that specific product list rather than category-wide.
  • Result: Executive dashboard (sales, profit, orders, customers) with drill-downs by category, region and segment.
